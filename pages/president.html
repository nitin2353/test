Message from the President

Syeda Taskeen Chishty

President

I, Syeda Taskeen Chishty, warmly welcome you as the President of AFTAB Women Welfare Society.

AFTAB Women Welfare Society is a dedicated non-profit organization committed to the empowerment and welfare of women. Our core focus areas include women’s education, health awareness, skill development, self-reliance, and promoting awareness about women’s rights. We firmly believe that empowering women is the foundation for building a strong and progressive society.

"As a social worker, my continuous effort has been to reach out to women and girls in need, providing them with guidance, support, and opportunities to lead dignified and independent lives."

Through our various initiatives and programs, the Society strives to create a positive and sustainable impact in the lives of women from all sections of society.

My vision is to see AFTAB Women Welfare Society emerge as a trusted and impactful organization that brings meaningful change and ensures respect, safety, and equal opportunities for every woman.

The trust and support of our members, volunteers, and well-wishers are our greatest strength. Together, let us work towards building an empowered, inclusive, and compassionate society.


Warm regards,

Syeda Taskeen Chishty
President
