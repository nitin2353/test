Our Initiatives

Transforming lives through comprehensive development programs focused on education, health, and self-reliance.

Women's Education

Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. At AFTAB Society, we believe that educating a woman is educating an entire generation.

We run evening schools and literacy drives for women who never got the chance to attend school, helping them read, write, and do basic calculations.

  • Basic Literacy Programs
  • Girl Child School Enrollment
  • Remedial Classes for Dropouts

Skill Development

Financial independence is the key to true empowerment. Our "Hunar Se Rozgar" initiative focuses on teaching practical skills that help women earn a livelihood from their homes.

We provide free training materials and machines to the most needy women, ensuring they can stand on their own feet.

  • Sewing & Tailoring Centers
  • Computer Literacy Workshops
  • Handicraft & Embroidery Training

Health & Hygiene

Rural women often neglect their health due to lack of awareness and resources. We organize regular medical camps to provide free checkups and medicines.

A major focus is on Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM), breaking taboos and distributing sanitary pads.

  • Free Medical Checkup Camps
  • Menstrual Hygiene Awareness
  • Maternal & Child Nutrition

Other Key Focus Areas

Legal Rights Awareness

Conducting workshops to educate women about their fundamental rights, laws against domestic violence, and how to seek legal aid.

Community Support

Providing ration kits, winter clothes, and emergency relief to widows and destitute women in times of crisis.

Environment & Sanitation

Promoting cleanliness drives (Swachh Bharat) and tree plantation within communities to ensure healthy living.
