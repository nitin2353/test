Driving Change, Empowering Lives

Our roadmap to building a society where every woman stands tall with dignity.

Our Mission

To empower women and girls from marginalized communities by providing accessible Education, critical Health Awareness, and vocational Skill Training. We aim to break the shackles of poverty and dependency, ensuring they have the tools to lead dignified, independent, and confident lives.

Our Vision

To see AFTAB Women Welfare Society emerge as a beacon of hope and a trusted organization that ensures Respect, Safety, and Equal Opportunities for every woman. We envision a society where gender does not dictate destiny, and every girl has the freedom to dream.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our every action.

Compassion

We serve with a kind heart, understanding the pain of the underprivileged.

Integrity

We maintain complete transparency in our funds, actions, and intent.

Inclusivity

We help women regardless of their caste, religion, or background.

Empowerment

We don't just give aid; we build capacity for long-term independence.

Our Strategic Objectives

To turn our vision into reality, we have set clear goals for the coming years:

  • To eradicate illiteracy among women in rural districts through free evening classes.
  • To establish self-help groups (SHGs) that foster entrepreneurship and savings.
  • To provide legal counseling to victims of domestic violence and social injustice.
  • To normalize menstrual health conversations and distribute sanitary kits.
  • To create a network of volunteers who act as guardians of women's rights in society.
