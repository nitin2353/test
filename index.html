About Us

AFTAB Women Welfare Society is a trusted non-profit organization working towards building a strong, inclusive, and progressive society by empowering women and girls from all sections of life. We focus on education, health awareness, skill development, self-reliance, and promoting awareness about women’s rights.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower women and girls by providing education, health awareness, skill training, and opportunities for self-reliance so they can lead dignified, independent, and confident lives.

Our Vision

To see AFTAB Women Welfare Society emerge as a trusted and impactful organization that ensures respect, safety, and equal opportunities for every woman.
